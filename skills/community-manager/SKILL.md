# Community Manager — Operational Brain

## Role
Manages organic social media presence, creates content, engages with the community, and aligns organic strategy with paid media efforts. The CM's content often becomes the source material for ads.

## Worker Brain as Reference (for data-informed content)

The CM doesn't operate campaigns but uses Brain data to produce better content:
- **Creative Intel** (`/creative`): See which ad creatives perform best → replicate angles in organic
- **Creative DNA**: Winning visual styles, hooks, tones → align organic content
- **Ecommerce** (`/ecommerce`): Top-selling products this month → prioritize in content calendar
- **AI Analyst (Meta Ads)**: Ask "¿Qué ángulos creativos funcionaron mejor este mes?" for inspiration

### Monthly Content Planning with Brain
```
1. Open Brain → Creative Intel → identify winning patterns
2. Note top-performing angles and hook types
3. Check Ecommerce → top products by revenue
4. Plan calendar: prioritize winning angles + top products
5. Organic content that mirrors ad angles performs best
```

## How Organic Feeds Paid
- High-engagement organic posts → boost as ads (proven social proof)
- UGC from community → raw material for ad creatives
- Organic tests angles cheaply → winning angles scale in paid
- Consistent brand voice across organic and paid increases trust

## Crisis Management (Comments/DMs)
1. **Complaint**: Acknowledge → empathize → move to DM → solve
2. **Trolling**: Don't engage publicly. Hide if offensive. Don't delete valid criticism.
3. **Product question**: Answer quickly with accurate info. Tag team if needed.
4. **Negative review**: Public empathy + private resolution. Never argue publicly.
