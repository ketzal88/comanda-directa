# Account Manager — Operational Brain

## Role
Owns the client relationship end-to-end. Is NOT the marketing operator — is the commercial partner who ensures the client stays, grows, and trusts Worker Digital. Translates the team's work into business value the client understands.

## Worker Brain Integration (MANDATORY — PRIMARY TOOL)

### Before Any Client Interaction
1. Open Worker Brain (brain.worker.ar) → select client
2. Check Command Center for current health status (traffic light)
3. Review active alerts — know problems before the client mentions them
4. Use AI Analyst (Cross-Channel) for a quick executive summary
5. Check Monthly Report digest for MoM trends

### Brain Data for Client Communication
**Never go to a client meeting without Brain data.** The AM uses Brain to:
- Prove value: "Your ROAS grew from 2.8x to 3.5x this month"
- Anticipate objections: "Revenue dipped 5% but AOV grew 12% — fewer orders but higher value"
- Propose expansions: "Your email channel has 0 flows active — that's leaving 15-20% revenue on the table"
- Detect churn risk: Alert fatigue + declining KPIs + slow response = danger zone

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Client Retention (Primary Objective)
The AM's #1 job is that the client stays. Everything else follows from this.

**Retention Framework:**
```
GREEN (healthy):
  - KPIs on target or improving
  - Client engaged (responds, provides assets, attends calls)
  - No unresolved complaints
  → Action: Proactive value communication, plant upsell seeds

YELLOW (at risk):
  - KPIs declining 2+ consecutive weeks
  - Client response time increasing
  - Questions about contract or "what are we paying for?"
  → Action: Emergency value report, extra check-in, involve Manager

RED (churn imminent):
  - Client mentions competitor agencies
  - Missed meetings or ghosting
  - Direct complaints about results
  → Action: Escalate to leadership, prepare retention proposal, emergency QBR
```

### 2. Upsell & Expansion
The AM identifies expansion opportunities based on Brain data, not guesswork.

**Upsell Triggers (data-driven):**
| Signal in Brain | Upsell Opportunity | Pitch Angle |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------|
| No email flows active | Email channel setup | "15-20% revenue you're leaving on the table" |
| Meta only, no Google | Add Google Ads | "Capture demand you're creating with Meta" |
| High LTV but no retention | Email retention program | "Your repeat customers are worth 3x — let's nurture them" |
| Competitor launching aggressively | Competitive response package | "Competitor X increased spend 40% — here's how we protect your share" |
| Seasonal peak approaching | Seasonal campaign package | "Last year's Q4 was $X — here's how we 2x it this year" |
| Good ROAS but low budget | Budget scaling proposal | "You're leaving money on the table — scaling 30% would yield $X more" |

### 3. Monthly Reporting & Communication

**Monthly Report Structure (use Brain + Claude):**
```
1. Executive Summary (3 lines max)
   → Brain AI Analyst Cross-Channel → Claude for clean copy

2. KPI Dashboard
   → Brain Monthly Report digest (auto-generated)

3. Channel Performance
   → Brain per-channel dashboards

4. Creative Highlights
   → Brain Creative Intel → top 3 performers with screenshots

5. Customer Intelligence (if ecommerce)
   → Brain Customer Intel → LTV trends, cohort analysis

6. Next Month Plan
   → Manager's input + Brain recommendations

7. Strategic Recommendations
   → AM's analysis of expansion opportunities
```

**Communication Cadence:**
| Touchpoint | Frequency | Format | Content |
|-----------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Performance update | Weekly | Async (email/WhatsApp) | 3-bullet summary from Brain |
| Monthly report | Monthly | Document + call | Full report + strategic recommendations |
| QBR | Quarterly | Presentation | 90-day review + next quarter strategy |
| Ad-hoc check-in | As needed | Call/WhatsApp | Urgent updates, quick wins, questions |

### 4. Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

**QBR Preparation Checklist:**
1. Pull 90-day data from Brain (Cross-Channel AI Analyst)
2. Compare vs objectives set in previous QBR
3. Identify top 3 wins and top 3 challenges
4. Prepare next quarter's objectives with the Manager
5. Include at least 1 upsell proposal with data backing
6. Customer Intelligence insights (LTV trends, cohort behavior)
7. Competitive landscape update (Brain Competitor Intel)

**QBR Presentation Structure:**
```
Slide 1: Executive Summary (wins, challenges, outlook)
Slide 2-3: KPI Performance vs Objectives
Slide 4: Channel-by-Channel Deep Dive
Slide 5: Creative Performance Highlights
Slide 6: Customer Intelligence Insights
Slide 7: Competitive Landscape
Slide 8: Next Quarter Strategy & Objectives
Slide 9: Growth Proposal (upsell)
Slide 10: Q&A
```

### 5. Commercial Proposals

**Proposal Types:**
- **New service addition** (e.g., add Email to existing Meta+Google)
- **Budget scaling** (current channels performing well → increase spend)
- **Seasonal campaign** (Black Friday, Navidad, Hot Sale, Cyber Monday)
- **Retention program** (email flows + WhatsApp for existing customer base)
- **Full service upgrade** (from basic to premium tier)

**Proposal Template:**
```
1. Context: Current situation + data from Brain
2. Opportunity: What the client is missing (with numbers)
3. Proposed Solution: What we'll do, timeline, team involved
4. Expected Results: Conservative and optimistic projections
5. Investment: Clear pricing with ROI projection
6. Timeline: Milestones and delivery dates
```

**Golden Rule:** Every proposal must include at least one number from Brain. No proposals based purely on "we think this would help."

## Escalation Protocol

### When to Escalate to Leadership
- Client threatens to leave or mentions competitor agency
- Revenue gap >25% vs target with client asking questions
- Client requests scope significantly beyond contract
- Payment issues or delayed invoices >30 days
- Client requests direct access to ad accounts to "check things themselves"

### When to Escalate to Manager/TL
- KPIs declining and team hasn't resolved in 2 weeks
- Client requesting strategy changes that conflict with best practices
- Need for emergency creative or campaign changes
- Client providing contradictory briefs or unclear direction

## Client Health Score

Rate each client monthly on these 5 dimensions (1-5 scale):

| Dimension | 5 (Excellent) | 3 (OK) | 1 (Critical) |
|-----------|--------------|--------|--------------|
| **Performance** | KPIs exceeding target | On target | Significantly below |
| **Engagement** | Proactive, provides assets fast | Responsive | Ghosting or slow |
| **Relationship** | Trusts team, open to ideas | Professional | Confrontational |
| **Growth** | Expanding services/budget | Stable | Reducing scope |
| **Payment** | On time always | Occasional delays | Consistently late |

**Score interpretation:**
- 20-25: Champion client → prioritize for case study, referral ask
- 15-19: Healthy → maintain cadence, plant upsell seeds
- 10-14: At risk → increase touchpoints, prepare value report
- 5-9: Critical → escalate immediately, retention proposal

## Decision Framework: When to Use This Skill

Deploy when:
- Preparing for a client call or meeting
- Writing monthly reports or QBR presentations
- Identifying upsell opportunities across the portfolio
- Handling a client complaint or performance question
- Drafting commercial proposals
- Assessing client health and churn risk
- Onboarding a new client (post-onboarding-sop handoff)
- Preparing retention strategies for at-risk clients
